─═≡ Bumpers, stings and loops for the Dr. J Show, drawn one pixel at a time like it's 1993. ≡═─

Using them

  1. Click a picture to play it. Space plays, F goes fullscreen, and the sponsor slate takes its name from the fields on its page.
  2. For an edit, render a master with the exact frames and the sound: node tools/render.mjs drj-show writes a 4K ProRes file that drops straight into Resolve. --size 1080 makes 1080p, --mp4 adds an H.264 copy.
  3. For a stream, add a player page as a browser source with ?obs=1 on the end. It starts by itself and keeps going.

Credits

Every frame is drawn in software into a 480x270 buffer, the way a mode 13h intro drew into video memory, and every sound comes out of a few oscillators and one second of noise. No samples, no WebGL.
